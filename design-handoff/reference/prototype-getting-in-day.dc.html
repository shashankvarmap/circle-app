THE SHORT VERSION
What we store: your number, name, handle, photo, notes, messages and who your friends are. Circle can't work without them.
Who can read it: only the people you've shared it with. Nobody at Circle browses your notes or chats. We look at something only if it's reported or the law requires it, and we record every time.
What we never do: sell your data, show ads, or build an advertising profile of you.
Companies that help us: a few providers run parts of Circle for us, like servers and text messages. They can only use your data to do that job.
Your rights: see it, fix it, download it, or delete it, in Settings, Your data.
