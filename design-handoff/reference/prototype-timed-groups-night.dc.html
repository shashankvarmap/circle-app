How timed groups work
For things that have an end.
A project, a trip, an exam, a wedding. Set an end date, and the group closes by itself. Nobody has to remember to tidy up.
1
Active
Works like any group. Everyone sees when it ends.
2
Ending soon
Reminders 3 days and 1 day before, with a Save a copy button.
3
Wrap-up: 7 days, read-only
Nobody can post. Everyone can save a copy. The host can extend it or make it permanent.
4
Deleted
Everything is removed from Circle for everyone, whether or not anyone saved a copy. Copies people saved stay on their own phones.
GOOD TO KNOW
Who can save a copy?
Every member, on their own phone, as a ZIP file. Nobody has to send anything to anyone.
What if I forget?
After the 7 days it's deleted. People who saved a copy keep it. Nobody else does.
Can it be changed?
The host can extend the end date or make the group permanent any time before it's deleted.
Can I leave early?
Yes. And you can delete your own messages any time.
